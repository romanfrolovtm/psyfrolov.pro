Self-check

Three tools you can use right now. The PHQ-9 and GAD-7 questionnaires are official, in the public domain, and used in clinical practice worldwide. Everything runs in your browser: answers are not sent anywhere and are not stored.

Important. These are screening tools, not a diagnosis. They indicate the likely severity of symptoms over the past two weeks and help you decide whether to seek support. Only a qualified professional can make a diagnosis after a proper conversation.
